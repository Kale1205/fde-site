LICENSE POLICY / IN DEVELOPMENT

Source code included. Freedom to customize, not to redistribute.

FDE IMS License is planned as a one-time, perpetual internal-use license with source code. The purchasing legal entity may inspect, edit, extend, and integrate it for its own internal business use. Intellectual-property ownership is not transferred.

Draft product policy. FDE IMS is under development and not yet formally on sale. Final commercial terms and the License Agreement / EULA will be presented before purchase is enabled.

Customization freedom ≠ redistribution rights.

01 / PRICE & SCOPE

Planned price and entity scope.

$313 one-time

The current planned price is $313 for a perpetual internal-use License. The final price and sale date are not yet confirmed.

1 License = 1 legal entity

Headquarters, branches, offices, and employees in the same purchasing legal entity are planned to be covered. Parent companies, subsidiaries, affiliates, and other separate legal entities generally require separate Licenses.

02 / PERMITTED USE

Internal operation and modification.

Internal use and source code

  • Perpetual use within the purchasing legal entity
  • Installation on necessary internal devices and internal backups
  • Inspection, editing, modification, extension, and internal integration of the source code
  • Internal use of modified versions

External developers

Source code may be disclosed as necessary to external developers working on the purchaser’s internal development, modification, or maintenance.

  • Confidentiality obligations are required
  • No reuse in other projects
  • No resale or redistribution by contractors
  • The purchaser must enforce the License conditions

03 / RESTRICTIONS

Rights that are not granted.

Sale and rights granting

  • No sale or resale of original or modified versions
  • No unauthorized transfer or sublicensing
  • No redistribution of source code, installers, or product files
  • No copying or sharing designed to bypass License terms

Publication and third-party services

  • No public repositories, websites, social media, or public file shares
  • No third-party SaaS, ASP, hosting, rental, or service-bureau provision
  • No business that lets third parties use FDE IMS itself

Using FDE IMS internally while selling the purchaser’s ordinary products or services is allowed.

04 / MAINTENANCE RESPONSIBILITY

License and Updates assign responsibility differently.

With License

The purchaser generally manages its implementation, functional updates, compatibility, and security. The source code and perpetual internal-use right remain with the purchaser under the License terms.

With Updates

FDE provides continuing functionality, security, and compatibility updates during the contract term. Source code is not provided under the Updates model.

05 / SWITCHING & CANCELLATION

How the two models are planned to connect.

License → Updates

The planned transition price is $31/month for the first 12 months, then the standard planned price is $62/month. The perpetual License itself is not extinguished.

Cancel after switching

Updates access and FDE-managed updates end with the subscription term. The previously acquired perpetual License remains, so the customer may continue under the License model with customer-managed updates and security.

Cancel Updates-only

The update service ends with the subscription term. Continued software use, data retention, and the version available after cancellation are not yet finalized.

06 / RIGHTS & NOTICES

Branding and intellectual property.

Internal customization of the logo, name, or UI is not planned to be categorically prohibited. Copyright, License, rights, and third-party notices in the source must be retained. Customers may not falsely claim that the original FDE IMS was independently developed by them.

07 / AI, OSS & THIRD PARTY

Separate terms still apply.

AI coding tools may be used for internal modification where submitted code is contractually or technically excluded from third-party model training. FDE IMS code may not be used for model training, fine-tuning, public datasets, or training intended to reproduce a similar product. OSS and third-party components remain subject to their own license terms.
