PLAN POLICY / IN DEVELOPMENT

Three plans, with clear rights and responsibilities

Choose a perpetual License, source-code License Plus, or FDE-managed Updates. The plans differ in source-code access, update responsibility, and term.

Draft product policy. FDE IMS is still in development. Prices below are planned and shown in USD; final commercial terms and the License Agreement / EULA will be presented before purchasing is enabled.

Internal use ≠ resale or redistribution

01 / PLANS & SCOPE

Choose how you want to own and maintain FDE IMS

License / $313 one-time

Perpetual internal use by one purchasing legal entity. Source code is not included. The first three months of Updates are included.

License Plus / $565 one-time

Perpetual internal use with source code and permitted internal modification. Updates are not included.

Updates / $75 monthly

FDE provides functionality, security, and compatibility updates during the active contract term. Source code is not included.

Each License or License Plus covers the purchasing legal entity. Parent companies, subsidiaries, affiliates, and other separate legal entities generally require their own plan.

02 / SOURCE & MODIFICATION

Source-code rights belong to License Plus

License and Updates

Source code and source-level modification rights are not included. Customers may use the software internally within the rights granted by the applicable plan.

License Plus

  • Inspect, edit, extend, and integrate the source for internal use
  • Use internally modified versions
  • Engage an external developer under confidentiality and the same restrictions
  • Retain copyright, license, rights, and third-party notices

03 / RESTRICTIONS

Rights that no plan grants

Sale and distribution

  • No sale or resale of original or modified versions
  • No unauthorized transfer or sublicensing
  • No redistribution of source code, installers, or product files
  • No copying or sharing intended to bypass plan terms

Publication and third-party access

  • No public repositories, websites, social media, or public file shares
  • No third-party SaaS, rental, hosting, or service-bureau provision of FDE IMS
  • No service that lets third parties use FDE IMS itself

Customers may still use FDE IMS internally while selling their ordinary products or services.

04 / UPDATE RESPONSIBILITY

Each plan assigns maintenance differently

License

FDE provides Updates for months 1–3. If the customer does not continue Updates afterward, the perpetual purchased version remains available and the customer manages its environment and ongoing operation.

License Plus

The customer manages source changes, functionality, compatibility, and security. No Updates entitlement is included.

Updates

FDE provides continuing functionality, security, and compatibility updates while the Updates contract is active.

05 / CONTINUATION & CHANGES

Planned continuation, upgrade, and cancellation rules

License Updates schedule

Months 1–3 are included. For months 4–9, Updates may be continued for $38/month. From month 10 onward, the standard planned Updates price of $75/month applies.

License → License Plus

A planned $252 one-time upgrade adds the License Plus source-code and internal-modification rights. License Plus does not include Updates.

No automatic paid conversion

Included or discounted Updates never convert automatically into a paid subscription. Ending Updates does not extinguish a previously purchased perpetual License or License Plus. An Updates-only right ends with its contract term.

06 / RIGHTS & NOTICES

Branding and intellectual property

For License Plus internal modifications, changes to the logo, name, or UI are not planned to be categorically prohibited. Intellectual-property ownership is not transferred, required notices must remain, and customers may not claim that the original FDE IMS was independently developed by them.

07 / AI, OSS & THIRD PARTY

Separate terms still apply

License Plus source code may be used with internal AI coding tools only where submitted code is contractually or technically excluded from third-party model training. FDE IMS code may not be used for model training, fine-tuning, public datasets, or training intended to reproduce a similar product. OSS and third-party components remain subject to their own licenses.

Deployment, hosting, backups, incident response, support SLA, tax, refunds, and delivery details remain under review and are not promised by this page.
