Ask about these terms

Internal use ≠ resale or redistribution

These products are for internal business use. Customers may use FDE IMS while selling their ordinary products or services, but may not sell, sublicense, host, or redistribute FDE IMS itself.

02Deliverables

License

Perpetual internal use by one purchasing legal entity. Source code and source-level modification rights are not included. The first three months of Updates-equivalent service are included.

License Plus

Planned for perpetual internal use with full source, internal customization, technical documentation, and customer-server/self-hosted operation. The License Updates add-on is not included or available for Plus.

03Internal use

What License Plus allows

  • Inspect, edit, extend, and integrate the full source for internal use
  • Use internally modified versions
  • Engage an external developer under confidentiality and the same restrictions
  • Use planned build, deployment, database, API, and backup/restore documentation

What no plan grants

  • No sale or resale of original or modified versions
  • No unauthorized transfer, sublicensing, or redistribution of product files
  • No public repositories, websites, social media, or public file shares
  • No third-party SaaS, rental, hosting, or service-bureau provision
04Updates and maintenance

License Updates

Months 1–3 are included. Optional continuation requires explicit opt-in at the $31/month USD candidate price for months 4–6 and $62/month from month 7. The purchased version remains available if Updates is not continued.

License Plus responsibility

The purchaser manages source changes, functionality, compatibility, and security. Updates is not a third product and cannot be added to License Plus.

05Conditions

Legal entity and notices

Each product covers the purchasing legal entity. Parent companies, subsidiaries, affiliates, and other separate legal entities generally require their own plan. Intellectual-property ownership is not transferred, and copyright, license, and third-party notices must remain.

AI, OSS, and self-hosting

Internal AI tools may be used only when submitted code is excluded from third-party model training. Model training and public datasets are prohibited. Self-hosting, supported environments, deployment, SLA, tax, refunds, migration, and delivery details remain under development or review.
