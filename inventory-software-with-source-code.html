SOURCE RIGHTS / LICENSE PLUS

Inventory software with source code: FDE IMS License Plus

License Plus is the planned FDE IMS option for a purchasing legal entity that needs to inspect and modify the product source for its own internal work.

Planned and in development · Source delivery and purchasing are not available

01 / QUICK ANSWER

Source access comes with boundaries

Receiving source code is different from receiving ownership of the intellectual property or permission to distribute the software.
01

Full source planned

License Plus is planned to include the FDE IMS source code and supporting technical documentation.

02

Internal change

The purchasing legal entity may use internally modified versions, subject to the final License Agreement and required notices.

03

Customer-managed

The purchaser manages source changes, functionality, compatibility, updates, and security. License Updates are not included.

02 / PLANNED SCOPE

What License Plus is intended to provide

The current $699 one-time USD amount is an unapproved international price candidate. The underlying product is still being built, and the final source package, documentation, delivery method, and system requirements are not complete.

CODE

Inspect and extend

Planned rights include inspecting, editing, extending, and integrating the full source for the purchaser’s own internal use.

DOCUMENTATION

Technical materials

Build, deployment, database, API, and backup/restore documentation are planned. Their final content remains in development.

EXTERNAL DEVELOPER

Controlled assistance

An external developer may be engaged under confidentiality and the same use restrictions. This does not permit public or general third-party access.

03 / RESTRICTIONS

Source included does not mean open source

Baked Kale FDE is planning access for internal modification, not a transfer of copyright or a right to commercialize FDE IMS as another product.

NOT PERMITTED

No resale or redistribution

  • No sale or resale of original or modified versions
  • No public repositories or public file sharing
  • No unauthorized transfer or sublicensing
  • No third-party SaaS, rental, hosting, or service-bureau use
PURCHASER RESPONSIBILITY

Changes stay with the customer

  • Manage internal modifications and their behavior
  • Manage compatibility, updates, and security
  • Keep copyright, license, and third-party notices
  • Apply separate terms for open-source and third-party components

FDE IMS License Plus is in development. The source package, installer, and customer distribution path are not available, and no delivery date is promised on this page.

05 / FAQ

Source-code questions

01Which FDE IMS product is planned to include source code?

FDE IMS License Plus is planned to include the full source code. The standard License does not include source code or source-level modification rights.

02Is FDE IMS License Plus open-source software?

No. The planned source-code access is for internal use by the purchasing legal entity. Copyright and license notices must remain, and public distribution is not permitted.

03Can a License Plus customer modify the code?

Internal modification is planned to be permitted. The purchaser will be responsible for its modifications, compatibility, updates, and security.

04Can the source code or a modified version be resold?

No. Resale, redistribution, unauthorized transfer, and sublicensing are planned to remain prohibited, including for modified versions.
