WHY FORWARD DEPLOYED ENGINEERING

Your team adapts to the software.
Why should that be the norm?

Check the stock. Copy it into another sheet. Hand it over to someone else. FDE starts with the work people actually do.

Start with “How much is in stock?”

Before counting features, understand how a team checks its stock. Scattered records are a design problem worth starting with.

Rethink copying and handovers

Bring repeated entry into one workflow. Explore receiving, transfers, counts, and shipping in the simplified FDE IMS demo.

Put the idea to the test

Try the interface and decide whether it fits. The demo uses sample data only; changes are not saved.

FDE IMS is in development. Demo availability does not mean the native product is released or available to purchase.
