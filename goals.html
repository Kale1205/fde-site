KALE’S GOALS

Recurring problems.Clear patterns.Practical products.

Baked Kale identifies common workflow problems in smaller companies, standardizes the solution, and delivers practical software designed to be straightforward to adopt and maintain.

WHO / METHOD / DELIVERY

01 / OPERATING MODEL

Who we serve, how we work, and what we deliver.

01

WHO

Smaller companies and sole proprietors

Teams facing recurring manual work, errors and rework, scattered tools, or repetitive processes that limit growth.

02

METHOD

Organize and productize

Discover recurring needs, standardize the workflow, productize the solution, and validate it against practical use.

03

DELIVERY

Practical desktop software with a clear path forward

Beginning with inventory management and extending to workflow digitalization, provide clear functions, demos and setup guidance, local-first operating patterns, and an ongoing update path where continued maintenance is needed.

02 / PRODUCT PRINCIPLES

Three decisions shape the product strategy.

  1. 01

    Start small

    Begin with operational areas where value can be measured quickly, such as inventory management.

  2. 02

    Standardize first

    Prioritize reusable product capabilities over one-off custom development whenever possible.

  3. 03

    Preserve customer control

    Use perpetual licensing and source-code access to leave room for long-term internal operation and extension.
