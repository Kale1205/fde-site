01 / What is FDE?

Understand the work.
Build what helps.

FDE stands for Forward Deployed Engineering: understanding how work happens on the ground, then turning that understanding into software that is useful there.

The starting point is the work, not the technology. Who does it? Where does time go? What gets in the way? Those questions guide a system people can keep using.

02 / What we do

A working system.
And the code behind it.

Business software should be easier to adopt. Baked Kale aims to lower that barrier by offering systems with their source code.

You do not have to start from zero, or reshape every process around an off-the-shelf tool. Start with a working system, understand how it works, and adapt it to your business. That is the choice we are building with FDE IMS License Plus.

03 / Our goal

Your software people.
Your partner, for the long run.

Delivering a system should not be the end of the relationship. As your work changes and new improvements become necessary, we want to remain someone you can turn to.

A system you can understand and develop yourself. A partner you can talk to when you need one. Keeping both within reach is what Baked Kale aims for.
