Kale’s Goals

Turn recurring operational problems
into products that are easy to adopt.

Kale’s FDE aims to identify common workflow problems in smaller companies, standardize the solution, and deliver practical desktop software that is straightforward to deploy and maintain.

Business Model

Who we serve, what we build, and how we deliver it

Approach

Build only what the operation actually needs.

01 / STANDARDIZE

Product

Turn common operational needs into standardized products with clear functions and pricing before purchase.

02 / DEPLOY

Deployment

Provide clear demos, setup guidance and local-first operating patterns so customers can get started quickly.

03 / SUSTAIN

Maintenance

Offer an ongoing update path for customers who want continued maintenance after the initial purchase.

Kale’s FDE Principles

Three principles behind the product strategy

01

Start small

Begin with operational areas where the value can be measured quickly, such as inventory management.

02

Standardize first

Prioritize reusable product capabilities over one-off custom development whenever possible.

03

Preserve customer control

Use perpetual licensing and source-code access to leave room for long-term internal operation and extension.
