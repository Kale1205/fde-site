02 / WORKFLOW

The operational problem FDE IMS is addressing

Small operations often need a dependable answer to a few basic questions: what is in stock, what came in, what went out, and what may be running low. FDE IMS is being shaped around that narrower workflow.

PAPER

Repeated handwritten records

A searchable record can reduce the need to find and reconcile separate notes. The final migration method from paper is not defined.

SPREADSHEETS

Files that drift apart

A shared workflow can clarify where stock movements are recorded. Supported import formats and migration services are not confirmed.

EXISTING SYSTEM

More complexity than needed

FDE IMS may be considered when a smaller workflow is the goal. Product fit must be assessed before assuming replacement is appropriate.

05 / FAQ

Small-business fit questions

01Who is FDE IMS being designed for?

FDE IMS is being designed for small businesses that want a clearer inventory workflow than repeated paper records or scattered spreadsheets.

02What inventory tasks can I see in the current preview?

The development preview uses sample data to show inventory search, receiving, shipping, and low-stock status. Preview changes are temporary and do not prove final product scope.

03Can Baked Kale FDE migrate our spreadsheet or existing-system data?

That is not yet confirmed. Supported file formats, data import, migration services, and implementation assistance will be defined before formal sales.

04Can a small business start using FDE IMS now?

No. FDE IMS is in development. The current site offers a temporary sample-data preview and contact form, but not purchasing, production deployment, or installer delivery.
