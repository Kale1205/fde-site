OWNERSHIP GUIDE / FDE IMS

FDE IMS as one-time purchase inventory software

FDE IMS is inventory management software being developed by Baked Kale FDE. Its current plan has two one-time products, with different source-code and maintenance responsibilities.

In development · Purchasing and installer delivery are not available

01 / QUICK ANSWER

What “one-time purchase” means here

The purchase model, update coverage, and product rights are separate decisions. FDE IMS keeps them explicit.
01

Two products

License and License Plus are the two planned products. Updates is a License-only add-on, not a third product.

02

Perpetual use

The current plan allows the purchasing legal entity to keep using its purchased version internally without an expiry date.

03

Pre-release

These are planned terms for a product still in development. Checkout, payment, fulfillment, and installer delivery remain unavailable.

02 / PRODUCT CHOICE

Choose ownership and responsibility

Both planned products are one-time purchases for internal use by one purchasing legal entity. The important difference is not only price: it is whether source code is supplied and who manages changes, updates, compatibility, and security.

LICENSE

$349 one-time candidate

Planned perpetual internal use without source code or source-level modification rights. The first three months of Updates are included.

LICENSE PLUS

$699 one-time candidate

Planned full source code, internal-modification rights, technical documentation, and customer-server/self-hosted operation. The purchaser manages updates and security.

UPDATES

Optional for License

Continued Updates are a $31/month candidate for months 4–6 and a $62/month candidate from month 7. They require opt-in and never start automatically.

RIGHTPerpetual internal use by the purchasing legal entity
NOT INCLUDEDResale, redistribution, transfer, or sublicensing
PRICE STATUSAll USD amounts are unapproved international price candidates

03 / BOUNDARIES

A purchase model, not a promise of every service

One-time purchase describes the planned license term. It does not mean that every future update, migration service, hosting task, backup, incident response, or support level is included.

LICENSE CONTINUATION

Updates remain optional

  • Months 1–3 are included with License
  • Continued Updates require an explicit choice
  • No automatic paid conversion
  • The purchased version remains usable if Updates stop
LICENSE PLUS RESPONSIBILITY

Source ownership is not transferred

  • Internal source modification is planned
  • The purchaser manages modified code, compatibility, updates, and security
  • Resale and redistribution remain prohibited
  • The License Updates add-on is not available for License Plus

FDE IMS is not currently for sale. Final prices, delivery details, and the License Agreement / EULA will be presented before purchasing is enabled.

05 / FAQ

One-time purchase questions

01Is FDE IMS planned as one-time purchase inventory software?

Yes. FDE IMS is being developed as two one-time products: License and License Plus. Purchasing is not available while the product remains in development.

02Does one-time purchase mean the purchased version expires?

No. The current plan provides perpetual internal use by the purchasing legal entity. The final License Agreement and commercial terms will be shown before sales begin.

03Are Updates required after buying License?

No. The first three months are included, and continued Updates require explicit opt-in. If Updates are not continued, the purchased version remains available for internal use.

04Can I buy FDE IMS now?

No. FDE IMS is in development. Public checkout, payment, installer delivery, and automatic fulfillment are not available.
